# Booster contents

Write 2–3 booster facts per set and verify them. A wrong slot count was the first error a
reviewer caught on the original sheet.

## Where to check

1. **mtg.wtf**: `https://mtg.wtf/pack/<set>-play` for Play Boosters, `https://mtg.wtf/pack/<set>-draft`
   for Draft Boosters (paper; `-arena` variants differ). It lists each slot's sheet and rates. The
   page is long: ask WebFetch for the slot structure, and read on with `offset` if it's cut off.
2. **Wizards' "Collecting <Set Name>"** article on magic.wizards.com: official wording for
   double-faced card guarantees, foil rates and the land slot.
3. Scryfall for which common lands exist (`set:xxx t:land r:common -t:basic`), so a tip like
   "Raucous Carnival is the W/R dual" names a real card.

## Play Boosters (Murders at Karlov Manor, 2024, onward)

14 playable cards:
- 6 commons + 1 slot that is a common about 98.5% of the time (Special Guest otherwise), so **7
  common slots**
- 3 uncommons
- 1 rare or mythic
- 1 land slot. In some sets it can be a common nonbasic land (Duskmourn: 10 common lands at 5%
  each on the standard land sheet). In others it is basic only (Bloomburrow).
- 2 wildcards of any rarity, one non-foil and one traditional foil

Totals per pack: 1–3 rares or mythics, 3–5 uncommons, 7–9 commons. The old sheet drew "6 commons
+ 3 wildcards", which is wrong.

## Draft Boosters (until Play Boosters replaced them)

15 playable cards: 10 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 rare or mythic, 1 basic land. About 1 pack in 3
replaces a common with a traditional foil of any rarity. Check set-specific rules, for example:

- Innistrad: Midnight Hunt and Crimson Vow guarantee at least two double-faced cards. The land
  slot holds the set's showcase basics. Also a double-faced helper card and a token, neither
  playable.

## Mixed drafts

Packs from different eras have different sizes (14 vs 15), so the wheel comes at different
picks. Play Booster rounds hold more rares (two wildcards), so expect more bombs in those rounds.
Write that only if it is relevant; the facts list is per set.
