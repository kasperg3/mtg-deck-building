## Import into Cardmarket
1. On Cardmarket, create a Wants list (Wants → Create list → Magic) named e.g. "World Shaper upgrades".
2. Paste the contents of [`world-shaper.cardmarket.txt`](world-shaper.cardmarket.txt) (below) into **Add Deck List** and press **Add**.
3. Open the list and use **Shopping Wizard** to find the cheapest combination of sellers.

The same text also works for TCGplayer Mass Entry.

```
1 Heroic Intervention
1 Sylvan Safekeeper
1 Lord Windgrace
1 Ramunap Excavator
1 Tamiyo's Safekeeping
1 Zuran Orb
1 Ob Nixilis, the Fallen
1 Conduit of Worlds
1 Retreat to Hagra
1 Tunneling Geopede
```
