## Import into Cardmarket
1. On Cardmarket, create a Wants list (Wants → Create list → Magic) named e.g. "Mutant Menace upgrades".
2. Paste the contents of [`mutant-menace.cardmarket.txt`](mutant-menace.cardmarket.txt) (below) into **Add Deck List** and press **Add**.
3. Open the list and use **Shopping Wizard** to find the cheapest combination of sellers.

The same text also works for TCGplayer Mass Entry.

```
1 Mindcrank
1 Kami of Whispered Hopes
1 Evolution Sage
1 Ruin Crab
1 Assassin's Trophy
1 Llanowar Wastes
1 Beast Within
1 Underground River
1 Yavimaya Coast
1 Sultai Charm
1 Evolution Witness
1 Heartless Act
```
